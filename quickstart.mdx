OxaPay is a cryptocurrency payment gateway that lets businesses receive payments, send funds, exchange assets, and access market data. Developers integrate through service-specific APIs, each authenticated with its own API key.

---

## What do you want to build?

Choose the workflow that matches the payment, payout, swap, or data integration you want to build.

## From payment creation to webhook

A merchant creates a payment and registers a callback URL. OxaPay tracks the payment lifecycle and sends webhook updates to the merchant backend when the payment state changes.
