Novixo is non-custodial cryptocurrency payment infrastructure. A business can receive, monitor, and consolidate blockchain payments while the owner keeps the Seed, private keys, and ownership of the funds. Developers integrate through the HTTP API. That API contract states it is a synthetic fixture, not an authoritative production specification. Transport details are documented in [API overview](/api-reference/overview).

## Core workflow

The owner prepares addresses, the developer creates a payment or a static address, and the payer settles on the blockchain. Collect is optional and still requires the owner's signature.
