---
title: "Plans, Credit, and fees"
description: "Novixo charges for plans and infrastructure usage. It does not take a percentage of incoming payments."
---

Novixo does not charge a percentage of incoming payment value. Revenue comes from subscription plans, infrastructure usage, additional Credit, advanced features, and Enterprise services. The sources name those categories and do not define prices, plan names, or feature lists.

Subscription cost, Novixo Credit, and blockchain Network Fees stay conceptually and financially separate.

## Plans

Subscription plans may define Address Bank capacity, supported networks, included Credit, feature availability, and service level. "May" is the source wording. No concrete plan is defined. No supported-network list is defined. No plan or billing operation exists in the API.

## Credit

Credit measures infrastructure usage, not payment value. Additional Credit can be purchased when included Credit is exhausted, without a plan upgrade. No purchase API, balance field, or Credit unit is defined.

Creating a payment is not necessarily a billable Credit event. Credit may be consumed by blockchain transaction detection, configured webhook delivery, and broadcasting signed Collect transactions. Reviewing Collect candidates does not consume Credit.

## Network Fees

Network Fees and Gas are external blockchain costs. They are not Novixo revenue. Funds held in Address Bank addresses belong to the business and are never part of Novixo's operating balance or revenue. See [Security and trust](/concepts/security).
